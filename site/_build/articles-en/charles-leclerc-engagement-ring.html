Alexandra Saint Mleux and Charles Leclerc: the engagement ring

Published 20 October 2026 · 6 min read · by Oneiro Paris

A striking solitaire diamond held on an exceptionally delicate pavé band. The engagement and union of Formula 1 driver Charles Leclerc and Alexandra Saint Mleux brought one of fine jewellery's most coveted silhouettes back into the spotlight: a generous oval diamond set on a slender, diamond-set band. Far from ostentatious excess, this ring reaffirms the timeless appeal of clean lines and focuses entirely on the stone's proportion and fire.

Anatomy of a timeless classic: the oval diamond on a pavé band

Photographs shared by the couple and reviewed by jewellery specialists reveal a deliberately refined setting. The centre stone is an oval-cut diamond secured by four subtle prongs, resting on a slender band accented with micro-pavé round brilliant diamonds.

This design choice relies on three key strengths:

  • Elongating the fingers: The oval cut is renowned for creating an elegant, elongating effect on the hand thanks to its length-to-width ratio.
  • Larger face-up appearance: Carat for carat, an oval diamond displays a larger surface area than a traditional round brilliant diamond, giving the appearance of a larger stone.
  • Contrasting volumes: The delicate band makes the centre diamond appear to float effortlessly above the finger.

Evaluating press estimates with a clear eye

As always with celebrity announcements, media reports circulated varying figures, estimating the stone between 5 and 12 carats with valuations ranging from 500,000 euros to well over one million.

It is worth remembering that these figures remain visual estimates based solely on photographs. Without an official gemmological report (such as GIA or IGI), neither the exact colour grade, clarity level nor precise carat weight can be verified. In bespoke jewellery, true distinction comes from the harmony of proportions, not an arbitrary figure in a headline.

Creating a bespoke interpretation

Recreating this refined balance does not require the outsized carats seen in Monaco. The art of bespoke design lies in choosing the right proportions:

An oval diamond between 1.20 and 2.50 carats, cut with a balanced length-to-width ratio between 1.35 and 1.45, achieves identical elegance on a real hand. Set on an 18k gold band measuring 1.6 to 1.8 millimetres, the piece preserves its featherlight delicacy.

Natural or lab-grown diamond: making the right choice

Depending on your priorities and budget, two clear paths exist:

With a natural GIA-certified diamond, carefully targeting colour (F-G) and clarity (VS1-VS2) delivers maximum brilliance without paying a premium for invisible distinctions, as outlined in our 4Cs diamond guide. With a certified lab-grown diamond, the price is divided by three or four for identical quality, placing a breathtaking 2-carat oval diamond comfortably within reach.

What to take from it

The ring chosen by Charles Leclerc and Alexandra Saint Mleux proves that simplicity remains the highest form of sophistication. An impeccably cut oval diamond on a delicate gold band never goes out of style.
