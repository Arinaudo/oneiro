Proposing at Christmas or New Year: the timeline to hold

Published 26 August 2026 · 4 min read · by the Oneiro house

A third of all proposals happen between 20 December and 1 January. And every year, in November, we receive the same messages: "is it still doable?". Here are the dates, without evasion, so you never have to ask.

The real timeline: 2 to 7 weeks of production

Every piece is made to order: sourcing the stone, signing off the design, 3D modelling, casting, setting, polishing. Allow 2 weeks for a simple setting with an available stone, and up to 7 weeks for a complex creation or a stone that has to be searched for precisely. Add a few days of exchanges beforehand, and the delivery.

The cut-off dates, concretely

  • Before 25 October — the comfortable zone. Everything is possible: a fully bespoke creation, a stone sourced to a written brief, several rounds on the 3D model, engraving. This is when the work is done well.
  • Until 15 November — still calm, provided you decide quickly on the stone. One or two proposals, approve, start.
  • Until 30 November — feasible on collection models with light personalisation: gold colour, size, diamond type. Less latitude on the design.
  • After 5 December — we no longer commit to a date for the 24th. We say so immediately rather than promising and disappointing. In that case, two honest options exist (see below).

These dates are for delivery within France. Allow a few more days internationally.

The models that hold the calendar

When time is short, it is better to start from an existing model and personalise it than from a blank page. DELIA (cushion and halo, from €2,650) and CYRA (pear solitaire with a pavé band, from €2,600) are safe bets that accept your gold, your carat and your stone type without lengthening the timeline. AMI, our trilogy, remains feasible if the centre stone is chosen quickly.

If you are starting late — two real solutions

The "proposal ring". You propose with a stand-in — a simple band, or nothing at all — and you design the real ring together, afterwards. More and more couples do this, and it is often the best scenario: they choose their ring, you keep the surprise of the moment.

The gift made tangible. We prepare something that makes the project real under the tree: the appointment already booked, a selection of stones, a first sketch. It slips into an envelope and says as much as a ring box.

And the ring size?

That is the variable that derails timelines, because it is thought about last. Deal with it now: the four methods that genuinely work, without raising suspicion, are set out in our dedicated guide. And resizing remains possible afterwards on most models.

Discretion is part of the job

Exchanges at the hours that suit you, a neutral parcel with no mention of jewellery, one single person in the know: just say so in your first message. We know how. Thirty minutes by video or in Paris is enough to know whether your date holds — and we will tell you frankly, including when the answer is no.
