The House

Oneiro, from the Greek for "dream".
The rest is very concrete.

Oneiro is a young Parisian jewellery house, born of one simple conviction: buying an important piece of jewellery should be a happy moment — not an intimidating ordeal in a hushed boutique, nor a blind bet on a catalogue photograph.

Our story

Why we created Oneiro

It all started with something we lived through ourselves: to buy a beautiful piece of jewellery, you had to choose between the intimidating luxury of the grand addresses — and prices that mostly pay for the window — or standardised production online, with no advice and no soul.

We wanted a third way: a house where every piece is designed in Paris and made by hand in a French workshop, where the stone is chosen criterion by criterion with someone who takes the time to explain, and where the price reflects the piece — not the address.

Oneiro lives online, by choice. Our clients meet us by video, receive photographs, films and certificates, follow the making of their piece, and we stay in touch long after delivery. It is a different relationship with luxury: more direct, more transparent, more human.

The workshop

Jewellery tradition, today's tools

Every creation is born twice: first as a 3D model, which you approve — proportions, setting, how it looks worn — then in the workshop, where our jewellers cast, set and polish by hand.

3D gives us precision and the freedom to be bold; the craftsman's hand guarantees the finish. It is that combination that lets us make genuine bespoke pieces at prices that stay within reach, from €1,000 to €7,000.

18 carat yellow, white or rose gold, platinum on request: we work only with noble materials, chosen to cross generations.

Our commitments

Ethics is not our selling point.
It is our production standard.

Many houses turn ethics into a marketing promise. We prefer to treat it as a given — included, verifiable, non-negotiable.

Traced stones

Untreated natural diamonds from conflict-free sources and compliant with the Kimberley Process, or lab-grown diamonds. In both cases: an independent GIA or IGI certificate, handed over with the piece.

Noble materials

18 carat gold and platinum, chosen for their durability as much as for their brilliance. Jewellery designed to be worn every day — and passed on.

Made in France

From the 3D model to the final polish, every piece is made in our workshop in France. A short supply chain, local craftsmanship, direct quality control.
