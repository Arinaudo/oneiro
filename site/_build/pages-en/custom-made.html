The process

How an Oneiro piece comes to life

The appointment

30 minutes by video or on WhatsApp. We talk about the project, the budget, the timing and your taste. You leave with an honest price range and the right questions.

The stone & the design

We shortlist 2 or 3 certified diamonds against your criteria and sketch the setting. Detailed, transparent quote, with no obligation.

The 3D & the workshop

You sign off the 3D model of your piece before production. Then the French workshop takes over: casting, setting, polishing — with progress photographs along the way.

The handover

Free, tracked delivery, in its box with the stone's certificate. And we are still here afterwards: resizing, care, advice.

Timing: allow 2 to 7 weeks depending on the complexity of the piece and the availability of the stones. A date you cannot miss (a proposal, a wedding, a birthday)? Tell us at the first appointment — we commit before we begin.

Bespoke that really is bespoke

At Oneiro, "customisable" is not a homepage slogan: it is the default way we make things. On every creation, you choose:

Shape (round brilliant, oval, pear, emerald, cushion and more), carat, colour, clarity. Natural or lab-grown diamond, white or fancy coloured — always certified.

Yellow, white or rose gold, in 18 carat. Platinum available on request for exceptional pieces.

Setting, stone mounting (claws, halo, pavé and more), proportions, inside engraving. You can start from a collection model and modify it, or start from nothing at all.

The stone's certificate is handed over with the piece, along with a manufacturing warranty, resizing where the model allows it, and care advice for life. The details are here.

Before you begin

The questions we are always asked

Our creations start at around €1,000 (a pendant with a lab-grown diamond) and most engagement rings sit between €2,000 and €5,000 all in. The quote is free, detailed, and commits you to nothing.

Same physical properties, same brilliance, same certification — and roughly 35 to 40 % difference in price. One is a geological treasure, the other a technical feat and an environmentally minded choice. We explain it all here, without dogma: the right choice is yours.

Resizing is possible on most models. On fine jewellery that has not been personalised, you have 15 days to change your mind. Bespoke pieces, made for you, cannot be exchanged — which is exactly why we validate every detail in 3D before the workshop starts.

We have several techniques depending on whether the surprise has to be kept — a ring sizer posted to you, the measurement of an existing ring, or a quiet word with someone close. We discuss it at the appointment.
