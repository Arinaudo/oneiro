The process

How an Oneiro piece comes to life

The appointment

30 minutes by video or on WhatsApp. We talk about the project, the budget, the timing and your taste. You leave with an honest price range and the right questions.

The stone & the design

We shortlist 2 or 3 certified diamonds against your criteria and sketch the setting. Detailed, transparent quote, with no obligation.

The 3D & the workshop

You sign off the 3D model of your piece before production. Then the French workshop takes over: casting, setting, polishing, with progress photographs along the way.

The handover

Free, tracked delivery, in its box with the stone's certificate. And we are still here afterwards: resizing on request, care, advice.

Timing: allow 4 to 6 weeks on average as an indicative timeframe depending on the complexity of the piece and stone sourcing. A date you cannot miss (a proposal, a wedding, a birthday)? Tell us at the first appointment: we commit before we begin.

Bespoke that really is bespoke

At Oneiro, "customisable" is not a homepage slogan: it is the default way we make things. On every creation, you choose:

Shape (round brilliant, oval, pear, emerald, cushion and more), carat, colour, clarity. Natural or lab-grown diamond, white or fancy coloured: always certified (GIA, IGI or HRD).

14k, 18k yellow, white or rose gold, or 950/1000 platinum.

Setting, stone mounting (claws, halo, pavé and more), proportions, inside engraving. You can start from a collection model and modify it, or start from nothing at all.

The stone's certificate is handed over with the piece, along with a manufacturing warranty against defects, resizing on request depending on the model and size difference, and care advice for life. The details are here.

Before you begin

The questions we are always asked

Each bespoke creation is individually quoted with a free, detailed and transparent estimate based on your chosen metal and centre stone characteristics (natural or lab-grown). A quote commits you to nothing.

Same physical properties, same brilliance, same certification standards (GIA, IGI or HRD). One is a geological treasure, the other a technical feat and a contemporary choice. We explain it all here, without dogma: the right choice is yours.

Resizing is available on request depending on the setting structure and size difference. Bespoke pieces crafted to order cannot be returned or exchanged: this is exactly why every detail is validated with you in 3D prior to workshop production.

We have several techniques depending on whether the surprise has to be kept: a ring sizer posted to you, the measurement of an existing ring, or a quiet word with someone close. We discuss it at the appointment.
