The guide

The 4Cs, explained simply

Four criteria are enough to describe: and to appreciate at the right price: any diamond. Here they are, without the jargon.

Carat: the weight

1 carat = 0.20 g. The higher the carat, the larger the stone. A tip: aiming just under the thresholds (0.90 instead of 1.00 ct) gives the same visual presence for a noticeably gentler price.

Cut: the craft

The way the stone is cut: proportions, symmetry, polish. This is the criterion that creates the sparkle. An "Excellent" cut maximises brilliance whatever the shape: round brilliant, oval, pear, emerald and more.

Colour

White diamonds are graded from D (perfectly colourless) to Z (a visible warm yellow tint). From D to H, the difference is imperceptible to an untrained eye: a well cut G or H is often the best balance of beauty and budget.

Clarity

Every stone carries small natural marks (inclusions). From FL (Flawless, purest in the world under 10x magnification) to SI (slight inclusions). The right balance: VS or VVS: imperceptible inclusions, exceptional fire.

The choice that shapes the budget

Natural or lab-grown: one stone, two stories

To the naked eye: and even under a loupe: nothing separates them. Both are real, certified, eternal diamonds. One is a geological treasure formed millions of years ago; the other a contemporary achievement, accessible at about 60 to 75% less.
