Delivery & timing

Free, tracked, everywhere

Delivery is free within France and internationally, with tracking. Outside the European Union, any customs duties and import taxes are payable by the recipient (French VAT is then not charged).

Since every piece is made to order or in very limited runs, allow 2 to 7 weeks between the order and delivery, depending on the complexity of the piece and the availability of the stones. If you have a date you cannot miss — a proposal, a birthday — tell us before you order: we confirm what is feasible before taking any payment, and we keep you informed if anything changes.

Returns & exchanges

15 days to change your mind on fine jewellery

Fine jewellery pieces from the collection may be returned within 15 days of delivery, provided they are unworn, in their original condition, with their packaging and their certificate of authenticity. Refunds are issued to the original payment method, after inspection of the piece.

Not returnable or exchangeable: loose diamonds. For bespoke pieces, we validate every detail with you (3D model, certificate, quote) before production begins.

Before sending anything back, contact us: we will give you the secure shipping procedure.

Warranty & care

After the purchase, we are still here

Every creation is covered by a 6 month warranty against manufacturing defects (excluding normal wear and impacts). Beyond that, our workshop handles repairs, repolishing and checks on stone settings, on quotation.

Resizing is possible on most rings, depending on the model and the setting (a fee may apply). If you are unsure about the size at the time of purchase, talk to us — there are good solutions, even when it has to stay a surprise.

And so the brilliance lasts: care instructions are handed over with the piece, and we remain reachable for any question, years later.

Certificates & materials

What the paper says — and why it matters

Every centre diamond comes with its independent GIA or IGI certificate, describing the stone precisely (carat, colour, clarity, cut). It is your proof of value — keep it, you will need it for insurance as much as for any future resale.

Our natural diamonds are untreated and sourced from conflict-free areas (Kimberley Process). Our lab-grown diamonds are certified to the same standards, marked "lab-grown". Our settings are in 18 carat gold (or platinum on request), made in France.

Payment

Secure, and flexible where needed

You can pay by card (Visa, Mastercard, American Express), PayPal, Apple Pay, bank transfer. Prices are shown in euros, inclusive of tax for the European Union.

For bespoke creations, payment is due once the quote and the 3D design have been approved — never before everything is clear and in writing.
