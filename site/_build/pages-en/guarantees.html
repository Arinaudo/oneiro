Delivery & timing

Free, tracked, everywhere

Delivery is free within France and internationally, with tracking. Outside the European Union, any customs duties and import taxes are payable by the recipient (French VAT is then not charged).

Since every piece is made to order or in very limited runs, allow 4 to 6 weeks on average as an indicative timeframe between the order and delivery, depending on the complexity of the piece and stone sourcing (an additional delay may occasionally occur depending on workshop schedules). If you have a date you cannot miss: a proposal, a wedding, a birthday: tell us before you order: we confirm what is feasible before taking any payment, and we keep you informed at every stage.

Returns & exchanges

Bespoke and made-to-order creations

Because all our pieces are handcrafted exclusively to order in our French workshop to your exact specifications, they cannot be returned, exchanged or refunded, in accordance with Article L.221-28 of the French Consumer Code on personalised and bespoke goods.

This is precisely why we validate every aesthetic and technical detail with you beforehand: accurate 3D modelling, meticulous stone shortlisting, and written quote sign-off prior to bench work.

Should you have any questions or require an adjustment following delivery, our team is at your complete disposal.

Warranty & care

After the purchase, we are still here

Every creation is covered by the statutory warranty against manufacturing defects and hidden flaws (excluding normal wear, accidental damage and mishandling). Beyond that, our workshop provides servicing, repolishing and stone setting checks on quotation.

Resizing is available on request only and depends both on the setting design (full eternity pave bands, for instance, cannot be resized) and on the size difference: if the difference is too large, the ring will need to be remade. If you have any doubt about ring size when planning your piece, let us know: we have proven methods to get it right from the start.

And so the brilliance lasts: care instructions are handed over with the piece, and we remain reachable for any question, years later.

Certificates & materials

What the paper says, and why it matters

Every centre diamond, whether natural or lab-grown, comes with its independent certificate (GIA, IGI or HRD), describing the stone precisely (carat, colour, clarity, cut). It is your proof of value: keep it, you will need it for insurance as much as for any future resale.

Our natural diamonds are untreated and sourced from conflict-free areas (Kimberley Process). Our lab-grown diamonds are certified to the same standards, marked "lab-grown". Our mountings are handcrafted in France in 14k gold (585/1000), 18k gold (750/1000) and platinum (950/1000).

Payment

Secure, and flexible where needed

You can pay by card (Visa, Mastercard, American Express), PayPal, Apple Pay, bank transfer. Prices are shown in euros, inclusive of tax for the European Union.

For bespoke creations, payment is due once the quote and the 3D design have been approved, never before everything is clear and in writing.
