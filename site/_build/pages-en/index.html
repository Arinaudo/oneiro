Our model

No boutique.
And that is excellent news.

No window on Place Vendôme, no six-figure rent passed on to your ring. Oneiro is an entirely online house, by choice: what we do not spend on a shop, we invest in the stone, the gold — and the time we spend with you.

I

The showroom is your living room

Video appointments, WhatsApp conversations, detailed photographs and films of every stone and every piece. You look, compare and decide from your sofa — with no salesperson leaning over your shoulder.

II

Workshop prices, not window prices

The same French craftsmanship and the same certified stones as the great houses — without the retail margins. Like for like, our pieces range from €1,000 to €7,000.

III

One person, from brief to box

From the first idea to secure delivery, the same person follows your project, shows you the progress and stays reachable after the purchase — care, resizing, advice.

How it works

From idea to jewellery box, in four steps

We talk

A 30 minute video appointment, free and with no obligation. Your project, your budget, your taste — and straight answers from us.

You choose the stone

Natural or lab-grown, we shortlist certified diamonds against your criteria, with photographs and certificates to back them up.

We create

3D modelling, design sign-off, then handmade production in our French workshop. You follow every stage in photographs.

You receive

Free, tracked delivery in a box worthy of the piece. Allow 2 to 7 weeks depending on the creation — we commit to the date before we start.
