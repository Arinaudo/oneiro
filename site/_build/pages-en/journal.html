Our method

Three rules we set ourselves

Numbers, not adjectives

When we talk about price, we give amounts and gaps. When we cite one of our own models, its price is shown. You can go and compare elsewhere: that is the point.

Advice before selling

Several of these articles explain how to spend less with us — aim under the carat thresholds, go down in colour, prefer lab-grown. That is deliberate.

Every piece mentioned is linked

Each article ends with the creations it talks about, with their prices. If we mention a full eternity band or a pear cut, the link takes you straight to the piece concerned.

A question you would like us to cover? Write to us — that is how half of these articles came about.
