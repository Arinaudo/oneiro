This English version is provided for convenience. In the event of any discrepancy, the French version prevails.

1. Data collected

When you are in touch with Oneiro (a quote request, an appointment, an order, a message), we collect the data you send us: name, email, telephone, delivery address and details of your project.

2. Use

This information is used solely to handle your requests and orders, for client service and — where you have consented — to send you communications from the House. It is neither sold nor passed to third parties beyond the providers strictly required (payment, delivery).

3. Cookies

This website uses no advertising tracking cookies. Only technical cookies strictly necessary for the site to work may be placed. You can manage them through your browser settings.

4. Retention & security

Your data is kept for as long as the commercial relationship and legal obligations require (invoicing, warranties), then deleted.

5. Your rights

Under the GDPR, you have a right to access, rectify, port and delete your personal data. To exercise it, write to us at contact@oneiroparis.com. You may also refer the matter to the CNIL (cnil.fr), the French data protection authority, if you believe your rights have not been respected.
