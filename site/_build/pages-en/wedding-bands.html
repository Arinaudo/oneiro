The styles

Plain, pavé or eternity

The plain band

A band in yellow, white or rose gold, with proportions adjusted precisely to your hand — width, thickness, court or flat profile, polished, brushed or hammered finish. Restraint, perfectly executed.

The pavé (half eternity)

Diamonds set across the visible half of the band — everyday brilliance, with added comfort. Natural or lab-grown diamonds, grain or claw setting.

The full eternity

Diamonds all the way round, like our tennis spirit on the finger. The statement piece, often chosen for a wedding anniversary.

The perfect match

Designed around your engagement ring

A wedding band is rarely chosen on its own: it stacks with the engagement ring, and sometimes nests into it to the millimetre (an open or curved band). If your ring came from us, we have its 3D files — the match is guaranteed. If it came from elsewhere, a photograph and a few measurements are enough.

And for the other hand in the couple: matching bands or deliberately different ones, inside engraving (a date, initials, words of your own), we create both together — with particular attention to the comfort of rings worn day and night.

In practice

Prices, timing, and how it works

Prices: since every band is made to your measurements, the price depends on the weight of gold, the width and any stone setting. Most plain bands stay well below the price of an engagement ring; pavé bands depend on the number and size of the diamonds. A precise, free quote within 48 hours — with no obligation.

Timing: 2 to 7 weeks depending on complexity. Wedding on a fixed date: we confirm what is feasible before you order and we hold the deadline — that is a commitment, not a sales promise.

The journey: a free video appointment, the choice of style and proportions, the 3D sign-off, then the workshop. Inside engraving free on request. And as always: free delivery, manufacturing warranty, resizing where possible.
