Advice appointment

Thirty minutes to give your project its shape

An appointment by video or in person in Paris, free and with no obligation, with the person who will follow your project from start to finish. No sales script: your questions, our straight answers — including "this model isn't right for you".

In practice

What happens during (and after) the appointment

We listen

Your project, the occasion, the budget, the taste of the person who will wear the piece. Send us your inspiration photographs or videos beforehand if you have any — or come empty-handed, that works too.

We explain

The 4Cs without jargon, natural versus lab-grown with the numbers in front of you, the traps to avoid, and an honest price range for your brief.

We propose

Within 48 hours you receive 2 or 3 concrete directions by email: shortlisted certified stones, design ideas, indicative quotes. You decide — or you don't. No insistent follow-ups.
