Why bespoke

The same budget, better invested

A catalogue ring makes you pay for a standardised design and for the window that displays it. With bespoke at Oneiro, the budget goes into the stone — which you choose criterion by criterion — and into the hours at the bench. And because everything is signed off in 3D before production, you see your ring before it exists.

Free advice

30 minutes by video: the project, the budget, the taste of the person you have in mind — and straight answers from us.

Stone & design

2 or 3 certified diamonds shortlisted within your budget, a sketch of the setting, a detailed quote.

3D & workshop

You sign off the exact model, then the French workshop builds it. Progress photographs throughout.

The big day

Free, secure delivery in its box with the certificate. Allow 2 to 7 weeks — the date is guaranteed before you order.

Budget

How much should you plan for? Let's talk numbers.

Forget the "three months' salary rule" — it is an advertising slogan, not a standard. In practice, in 18 carat gold with a certified diamond: from around €1,000 for a solitaire with a lab-grown diamond, €2,000 to €3,500 for the heart of the range (our AMI trilogy: €2,100 lab-grown, €3,300 natural), and above that for exceptional pieces. The natural or lab-grown decision alone accounts for a 35 to 40 % difference on the stone.

Keeping the secret (and getting the size right)

The size, without raising suspicion

An existing ring measured discreetly, a quiet word with someone close, or a ring sizer posted to you — we have a method for every situation. And resizing remains possible afterwards on most models.

Confidential exchanges

Appointments at the hours that suit you, a neutral parcel with no mention of jewellery, and one single person aware of the project: us.

Proposal planned for a fixed date? Say so at the first appointment: we confirm what is feasible before any commitment, and we hold the deadline.
