Ethical engagement ring: what does it really cost in 2026?

Published June 2026 · 5 min read · by the Oneiro house

Talking about price without evasion is rare in jewellery. Yet it is the first question you ask yourself — so here it is, answered plainly. Realistic ranges, what moves the bill, and where your money actually goes.

Forget the "three months' salary" rule

That "rule" is an advertising slogan from the 1980s, not a standard. The right budget is the one you can commit to with peace of mind. In France, most engagement rings sit between €1,500 and €5,000 — and a beautiful ring exists at each of those levels, provided the trade-offs are made well.

The realistic ranges (18 carat gold, certified diamond)

  • €1,000 – €2,000: an elegant solitaire with a lab-grown diamond (around 0.5 to 1 ct depending on quality), or a smaller natural stone. This is the entry ticket to genuine bespoke jewellery — at Oneiro, the first creations start at around €1,000.
  • €2,000 – €3,500: the heart of the market. A well cut natural stone of around 0.5-0.8 ct, or a generous lab-grown one (around 1-1.5 ct), with worked settings (halo, trilogy). Our AMI trilogy: €2,100 lab-grown, €3,300 natural.
  • €3,500 – €7,000: a natural stone of around 1 ct or more in fine quality, complex designs, pavé work, coloured stones (fancies). The territory of exceptional pieces.

Indicative ranges — every configuration is quoted precisely and free of charge.

What moves the price (in order of impact)

1. The centre stone — 60 to 75 % of the total price. Hence the importance of well arbitrated 4Cs: aiming under the carat thresholds, colour G-H, clarity VS, can mean up to 30 % difference for equal beauty. 2. Natural or lab-grown — around 35-40 % difference on the stone (our honest comparison). 3. The setting — a pared-back solitaire takes fewer hours at the bench than pavé work or a trilogy. 4. The distribution channel — see below.

Where your money goes: the part nobody itemises

In a ring bought in a traditional boutique, you pay for the stone, the gold, the craftsmanship… and a substantial share of commercial rent, sales staff and marketing. We made the opposite structural choice: no boutique, and what retail spends on the window, we give back in stone, craftsmanship and guidance. For the same budget, the difference shows on the finger — and it is verifiable by comparing like for like on certified characteristics.

And does "ethical" cost more?

No — and be wary if anyone charges it as an extra. 18 carat gold, traced diamonds from conflict-free areas (Kimberley Process), an independent GIA/IGI certificate: at a serious house, that is the production standard, included, not a premium option. The lab-grown alternative actually brings the bill down.

Three pieces of advice before you begin

  • Set the budget before looking at stones — doing it the other way round always leads to drift.
  • Insist on the independent certificate and on a quote itemised by stone, setting and craftsmanship.
  • Talk to a human being — 30 minutes of advice avoids the two classic mistakes: overpaying for clarity, and neglecting the cut.
