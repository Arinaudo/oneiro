Sofia ring – Asscher diamond, pavé & 18K gold. A perfect balance between modernity and timelessness. This ring elevates a 2 carat Asscher cut diamond, whose infinite facets capture light with spellbinding depth. Set in an 18 carat gold bezel, the stone seems to merge with the setting, sitting delicately on the finger for an absolutely fluid wear. A meticulous pavé of diamonds underlines the brilliance of the piece, creating a subtle play of reflections between gold and light. Its low, structured design, at once architectural and refined, gives this ring an assured elegance, made to travel through time with grace. Designed and made in France with exceptional craftsmanship, this piece embodies a contemporary vision of luxury, between power and delicacy.
