Terms and conditions of sale

This English version is provided for convenience. In the event of any discrepancy, the French version prevails.

1. Purpose

These terms and conditions of sale set out the rights and obligations of Oneiro and its clients in connection with the sale of jewellery and diamonds.

2. Products & personalisation

Every piece is made with care to the highest jewellery standards. Photographs and descriptions are provided for guidance and may vary slightly given the handmade nature of the creations. Personalised pieces (engravings, bespoke orders, engagement rings) cannot be returned or exchanged.

3. Prices & payment

Prices are shown in euros, inclusive of tax for orders within the European Union and exclusive of VAT for international orders. Payment is due in full at the time of the order. Payments are secure and accepted by card (Visa, Mastercard, American Express), bank transfer, PayPal, Apple Pay and Klarna (payment in 3 instalments).

4. Delivery & customs

Delivery is free within France and internationally, with tracking. Manufacturing and delivery times are 2 to 7 weeks depending on the piece. For orders outside the EU, customs duties and import taxes may apply and are payable by the client. Oneiro is not liable for delays caused by customs formalities.

5. Withdrawal, returns & exchanges

Under article L221-18 of the French Consumer Code, you have a statutory withdrawal period of 14 days from delivery. Oneiro extends this period to 15 days for fine jewellery pieces, returned unworn, in their original condition, with their packaging and certificate of authenticity. After inspection, the refund is issued to the original payment method.

Under article L221-28 of the French Consumer Code, the right of withdrawal does not apply to goods made to the consumer's specifications or clearly personalised: engagement rings configured to order, personalised creations (engraving, design modification, a stone selected on request).

Resizing is possible depending on the model and the extent of the change; a fee may apply. Please contact us before sending anything back.

6. Warranties & repairs

All our pieces benefit from the statutory warranty of conformity (articles L217-3 et seq. of the French Consumer Code, 2 years) and the warranty against hidden defects (articles 1641 et seq. of the French Civil Code). Oneiro additionally offers a 6 month commercial warranty against manufacturing defects, excluding normal wear, impacts or misuse. A repair service is available on quotation.

7. Intellectual property

All designs and content on the Oneiro website are protected and remain the exclusive property of the brand.

8. Applicable law & disputes

These terms are governed by French law. In the event of a dispute, an amicable solution will be sought before any legal action.
